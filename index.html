GENERAL RELATIVITY How Spacetime Bends Light

In flat space, light travels along straight lines. Around mass $M$, spacetime curvature curves light trajectories according to the null geodesic equation: $$\frac{d^2 \mathbf{r}}{d\lambda^2} = -\frac{3 r_s}{2 r^5} |\mathbf{r} \times \mathbf{v}|^2 \mathbf{r}$$ Notice how rays looping around the photon sphere ($1.5 r_s$) produce dual images of the rear accretion disk over the top and bottom!
